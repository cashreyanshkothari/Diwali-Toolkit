Never paste these into any AI app

  • Passwords, OTPs, card numbers, UPI PINs or net-banking details
  • Full PAN, Aadhaar or bank account numbers of you, your staff or customers
  • Customer names and phone numbers, unless the task truly needs them

AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Check every figure, and speak to your CA before acting on tax, GST or legal points.
